One board for the whole fleet

Gross and net for the period, rate per mile, who is loaded and who is empty — plus a day-by-day timeline of every truck: where it sat, where it went, when it frees up.

Rate con in, load out

Drop the PDF or a photo of the rate confirmation. The system reads the broker, the rate, the stops, the dates and the miles, then fills the load in by itself.

Profit known before we book

Every truck carries its own numbers — fuel mileage, driver pay, truck payment, insurance, permits. Attach a load and you see rate per mile, costs and what is actually left.

Live tracking from the ELD

The whole fleet on one map: who is driving, who is on duty, who is parked, and the miles left to delivery. A link to a single truck can go to a customer — they see that truck only.

Documents that file themselves

Rate cons, BOLs, PODs, registrations and insurance sorted by truck and by load. A driver photographs a document on the phone and it lands on the right load.

Broker check before the wheels turn

An MC number goes into the FMCSA registry check: is the authority active, is the insurance on file, how long in business — next to our own record of how they paid last time.

Tolls priced for a truck

Route options with toll roads costed for a five-axle rig, so the price of the road sits in the load's numbers instead of showing up as a surprise at the end of the month.

Invoicing, and who owes what

A delivered load becomes an invoice with the BOL and POD attached in one PDF. The list shows what is invoiced, what is paid, and what is overdue — by how many days.

What it gives our drivers

  • Pickup address, contacts and appointment times one tap away — no calling dispatch to ask.
  • Photograph the BOL or POD from the phone; it attaches itself and nobody chases you for paperwork.
  • Pay is figured from the same load numbers you can see, so settlement day holds no surprises.
  • Documents with an expiry date — CDL, medical card — get flagged before they lapse.

What it gives our customers

  • A tracking link for your load, showing that truck on a live map — no account, no login.
  • ETAs computed from the truck's own position and hours, not from an optimistic guess.
  • The invoice packet arrives complete the first time — invoice, rate con, BOL and POD in one PDF.
  • One dispatcher who has the whole load history in front of them when you call.
